AdAura Signs & Prints pricing policy — Phase 3
All supplier rates are stored GST-inclusive. The prototype uses a blanket 18% GST assumption for every product and variant, as requested. This is a business configuration for the prototype, not a determination that every product is legally taxable at 18%. Confirm product classifications and statutory invoicing treatment with your CA before accepting live orders.
B2C — confirmed policy
Apply a 100% markup to the supplier rate before GST, then add 18% GST.
Supplier cost before GST = supplier GST-inclusive rate / 1.18.
B2C selling price before GST = supplier cost before GST × 2.
Output GST = B2C selling price before GST × 18%.
Customer price = selling price before GST + output GST.
Example:
Supplier rate including GST: ₹118
Supplier cost before GST: ₹100
B2C selling price before GST: ₹200
Output GST (18%): ₹36
B2C customer price including GST: ₹236
This is a 100% markup on pre-GST cost, equivalent to a 50% gross margin on the pre-GST selling price before overheads and other costs.
B2B
Retain the initial configurable rule: supplier inclusive rate + max(20% of supplier inclusive rate, ₹50).
Implementation safeguards
The 18% assumption is enforced in the prototype migration. Final quotes and totals must be recalculated by a trusted server-side function. Browser-side values are display previews only.
